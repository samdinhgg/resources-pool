You can gather resources from different vendors as a Partner since you're getting a `@google.com` email. And we can collaborate here on this repo, utilizing Github Project or similar mechanisms. Because: one can't go through all the partner portals, we can split and allocate work to team members and pool a single "warehouse" of resources in this repo.

P/S: Public resources/data only. No corp or NDA data here.

## The Story

Google created and open-sourced Kubernetes (K8s) which is a Container Orchestration Platform.

You may also know that Google has an in-house live-long K8s version, bigger, stronger and globally scale, Borg.

I once questioned, how does Borg allocate services including GCE if it's a container orchestration platform (kind of), and from there we have a new concept: VM as a container, in other, VM are managed as container, not like a virtualization hypervisor (Type 1/Type 2).

I then encountered Harvester since it's a small-scale version of this concept, open-source and easy to access, solely built for HCI (Hyper-Converged Infrastructure), and we can quickly capture the idea behind of the concept, even though at a very much smaller scale than Borg/Google have.

Harvester is being maintained by SUSE, that's why I came there, to their Partner Portal, and that's why we have this repo.

P/S: A concept you may already know: Warehouse-Scale Computing.
