# Veeam Sales Professional VMSP 2025

- https://veeam.csod.com/ui/lms-learner-playlist/PlaylistDetails?playlistId=6e9ce15b-53ae-4bf7-8b81-c1429a112edb

Complete the mandatory modules and assessment in this training as a prerequisite to earning a VMSP badge for any of the VMSP competencies, including VMSP SaaS Protection, VMSP Cybersecurity & DR, VMSP Public Cloud, VMSP Kubernetes Data Protection, and VMSP Cloud Service Provider. For example: complete VMSP Veeam Fundamentals + complete VMSP SaaS Protection = earn the VMSP SaaS Protection badge. Earn a badge for each additional competency you wish to complete.
