# Veeam Sales Professional Veeam Fundamentals 2025

**VMSP Veeam Fundamentals** provides a holistic view of Veeam including what we sell, what we protect, and what's included. There will be a focus on the current landscape for customers, the challenges they face when protecting their data and how Veeam Data Platform & Veeam Data Cloud can solve their issues. Optional modules and content are available for additional support. Optional modules and content are available for additional support.

Complete the mandatory modules and assessment in this training as a prerequisite to earning a VMSP badge for any of the VMSP competencies, including VMSP SaaS Protection, VMSP Cybersecurity & DR, VMSP Public Cloud, VMSP Kubernetes Data Protection, and VMSP Cloud Service Provider.

For example: complete VMSP Veeam Fundamentals + complete VMSP SaaS Protection = earn the VMSP SaaS Protection badge. Earn a badge for each additional competency you wish to complete.
