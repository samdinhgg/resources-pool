In this challenge lab, you will act as a Lead Cloud Security Architect for Cymbal Bank. The organization has recently deployed a new "Secure Landing Zone" for its payment processing application, but a third-party security audit has flagged multiple critical misconfigurations and deviations from the Google Cloud Well-Architected Framework.
