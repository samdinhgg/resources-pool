## Build a Multimodal GenAI Pipeline with BigQuery: Challenge Lab

The objective of this challenge lab is to test your proficiency in building end-to-end "Data to AI" pipelines using BigQuery and Gemini. Additionally, emphasis is placed on operationalizing multimodal data processing and semantic search utilizing BigLake and Vertex AI.

### Lab - [Build a Multimodal GenAI Pipeline with BigQuery: Challenge Lab](https://partner.skills.google/course_templates/1574/labs/630182)

In this advanced capstone challenge lab, you will act as a Lead Data Engineer and AI Architect for Cymbal Shops. You are tasked with modernizing the company's legacy product catalog by building an end-to-end "Data to AI" pipeline. You will leverage the full spectrum of Google Cloud's Data to AI capabilities—starting from data governance with BigQuery Universal Catalog, moving to multimodal data processing with BigLake and Gemini, orchestrating workflows with BigQuery Pipelines, and finally deploying predictive models via Vertex AI. This lab tests your ability to unify structured and unstructured data, operationalize AI within the data warehouse, and deploy scalable machine learning solutions without moving data.
