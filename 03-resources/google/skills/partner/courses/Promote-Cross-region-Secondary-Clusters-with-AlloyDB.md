# [Promote Cross-region Secondary Clusters with AlloyDB](https://partner.skills.google/course_templates/1150)

**Description:**

This course dives deep into AlloyDB's advanced features and configurations, focusing on high availability and cross-region management. Through practical, challenge-based lab, participants will develop practical skills in setting up and managing AlloyDB clusters. The course is designed for those who seek to master complex database environments and ensure high performance and reliability across different regions.

## Promote Cross-region Secondary Clusters with AlloyDB

This module focuses on advanced AlloyDB configurations aimed at achieving high availability and effective cross-region management. Participants will explore the complexities of creating and maintaining robust AlloyDB environments. The module is structured around a challenge lab where participants are tasked with critical operations, including setting up a regional high availability AlloyDB cluster, establishing a secondary cluster with read pool capabilities, and promoting the secondary cluster to function as an independent environment. The module aims to enhance participants' ability to manage and configure AlloyDB clusters under real-world conditions.
