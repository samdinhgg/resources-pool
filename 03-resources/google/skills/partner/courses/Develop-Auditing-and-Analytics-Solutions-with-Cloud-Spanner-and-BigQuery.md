# [Develop Auditing and Analytics Solutions with Cloud Spanner and BigQuery](https://partner.skills.google/course_templates/1155)

**Description:**

This course explores advanced techniques for developing and managing data solutions using Google Cloud's Cloud Spanner and BigQuery. Participants will gain hands-on experience with building and administering complex business processes that leverage the scalability and power of these tools. Through challenge-based lab, learners will develop practical skills in database management, data analytics, and federated querying to enhance business intelligence and operational efficiency.

## Develop Auditing and Analytics Solutions with Cloud Spanner and BigQuery

This module focuses on integrating Cloud Spanner and BigQuery to develop comprehensive auditing and analytics solutions. Participants will engage in a challenge lab designed to test their skills in building and administering a business process using these powerful tools. The tasks include creating and managing Cloud Spanner instances, setting up databases and tables, performing backups and restorations, establishing federated connections in BigQuery, and creating views for data analysis. Participants will also review SQL executions from BigQuery within Cloud Spanner. This module aims to provide practical experience in linking database and analytics solutions to support business processes.

### Lab - [Develop Auditing and Analytics Solutions with Cloud Spanner and BigQuery: Challenge Lab](https://partner.skills.google/course_templates/1155/labs/505071)

In this lab, the user is tasked to build a proof of concept environment to parallel an existing production environment while creating an analytics reporting environment for the audit department.
